Privacy policy

This policy explains the information this website may collect when you contact the foundation.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice and must be reviewed by the foundation before publication. Update it to match the services, form provider, payment provider, analytics and hosting actually used.

Information you provide

If you contact the foundation or submit a volunteer enquiry, you may provide your name, email address, phone number and message. Share only information needed to respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information submitted through contact channels may be used to respond to enquiries, coordinate volunteer interest, discuss partnerships and maintain necessary records.

Payments

This website does not currently collect or store card, bank or UPI credentials. If a payment gateway is connected later, its provider’s privacy terms and data handling must be disclosed here.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising trackers are included in this starter build. If added later, disclose the services used and provide any legally required controls.

Sharing and retention

Do not sell personal information. Access should be limited to people who need it to respond to the enquiry or fulfil a legitimate administrative purpose. Set a retention schedule and deletion process before launch.

Your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent, contact info@tbr.net.in.
