About the foundation

Built around trust, care and collective action.

Trust Bridge Foundation is based in the Canning area of West Bengal. We believe meaningful progress starts when people can connect, contribute and work together.

We are building this space to make it easier to understand the foundation, reach our team and support its work. Our programme information and official documents will be added as they are approved for publication.
